How It Works

Three steps from LUS image to risk score

1

Upload LUS Image

Drag and drop a neonatal lung ultrasound frame captured at the bedside.

2

AI Analysis

MobileNetV3-Small + ClinicalMLP late fusion model processes image and vitals.

3

Get Risk Score

Receive Normal / Moderate RDS / High RDS classification with confidence scores.

Phase 1 — Binary Screen

Upload a LUS image. The CNN classifier (MobileNetV3-Small) gives a fast Normal / Abnormal verdict in under a second.

Try Phase 1 →

Phase 2 — Full Triage

Combine the LUS image with 8 clinical vitals for a 3-class RDS severity assessment: Normal, Moderate RDS, or High RDS.

Try Phase 2 →
